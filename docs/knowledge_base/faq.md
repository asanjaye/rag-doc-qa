## Product Questions

### Q: Does DataSync Pro support real-time synchronization?
Yes! DataSync Pro supports real-time synchronization with latency under 500 milliseconds for all supported connectors. This uses change data capture (CDC) technology to detect and sync changes instantly.

### Q: What databases does DataSync Pro support?
DataSync Pro supports PostgreSQL 12+, MySQL 8.0+, MongoDB 5.0+, Snowflake, and BigQuery. Enterprise customers can request custom connector development for other databases.

### Q: Can I try DataSync Pro before purchasing?
Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial with full access to Professional tier features. No credit card required. Sign up at trial.novatech.example.

### Q: Is there an on-premise deployment option?
On-premise deployment is available exclusively for Enterprise tier customers. Contact sales@novatech.example for details and pricing.

### Q: What happens if a sync fails?
DataSync Pro automatically retries failed syncs with exponential backoff (up to 5 retries over 24 hours). Failed records are quarantined in an error queue for manual review. You'll receive alerts via your configured channels (email, Slack, webhook, or PagerDuty).
