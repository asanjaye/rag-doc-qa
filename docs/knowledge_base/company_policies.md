# NovaTech Solutions - Company Policies

## 1. Remote Work Policy

NovaTech Solutions supports flexible remote work arrangements for all eligible employees.

### Eligibility
- Full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period
- Roles that can be performed effectively without physical presence
- Employees must have reliable internet (minimum 50 Mbps download)

### Core Hours
All remote employees must be available during core hours: **10:00 AM - 3:00 PM** in their local timezone. Outside core hours, employees have flexibility in scheduling their remaining work hours.

### Equipment
- NovaTech provides a laptop and one external monitor
- $500 annual stipend for home office equipment
- IT support available via Slack #it-help or helpdesk@novatech.example

### Time Tracking
Remote employees must log hours in the TimeTracker app by end of each workday. Minimum expected availability is 40 hours per week for full-time roles.

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

### Accrual Rates
- **0-2 years tenure**: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- **2-5 years tenure**: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- **5+ years tenure**: 25 days per year (2.08 days/month)

### Requesting PTO
1. Submit requests via the HR Portal at least 2 weeks in advance
2. Manager approval required for requests over 3 consecutive days
3. December 15 - January 5 is a blackout period requiring VP approval

### Carryover
Employees may carry over up to 5 unused PTO days to the following year. Days beyond 5 are forfeited on January 1st.

## 3. Expense Reimbursement

### Eligible Expenses
- Business travel (flights, hotels, ground transport)
- Client entertainment (pre-approved, max $150/person)
- Professional development (conferences, courses - max $2,000/year)
- Required software licenses not provided by IT

### Submission Process
1. Submit expenses within 30 days via Expensify
2. Attach itemized receipts for all expenses over $25
3. Manager approval required for expenses over $500
4. Reimbursement processed within 10 business days

### Non-Reimbursable
- Personal travel extensions
- Alcohol (except at approved client events)
- First-class airfare (economy or business only)
- Gym memberships (covered separately under wellness program)

## 4. Code of Conduct

### Professional Standards
- Treat all colleagues with respect and dignity
- Maintain confidentiality of proprietary information
- Report conflicts of interest to HR immediately
- No discrimination or harassment of any kind

### Communication Guidelines
- Use professional language in all written communications
- Respond to internal messages within 24 business hours
- Keep Slack status updated when away from desk
- Video on for meetings with clients and leadership

### Violations
Violations of the code of conduct may result in:
1. Verbal warning (documented)
2. Written warning
3. Performance improvement plan
4. Termination

Report violations confidentially to ethics@novatech.example or the anonymous hotline: 1-800-555-ETHICS.
